Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Sogdia ("the App") is published by DriftforgeDev, an independent developer based in the Dominican Republic. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information we collect

Sogdia does not require an account and does not collect, store or sell any personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts or payment details. The App does not use analytics or tracking tools and does not set its own cookies.

2. Hosting

The App's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure, according to the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use this data.

3. Automatic translation

To show the App in your language, the App's text (store names excluded) and your language code are sent to Google's translation service (translate.googleapis.com). No personal information is included, although, as with any web request, Google receives your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data stored on your device

The App saves translations in your device's local storage, and a copy of its pages in the browser cache, so it loads faster and works when you are offline. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

5. Affiliate links

Sogdia shows links to third-party stores and couriers. Some of these are affiliate links: if you make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When you open a store, that store and the affiliate network it uses (for example Admitad, Involve Asia or INRDeals) may use cookies or similar technologies to record the visit and the purchase, according to their own privacy policies. Purchases, payments, shipping and returns are handled entirely by each store.

6. Advertising

Sogdia does not show third-party ads inside the App and does not use the advertising ID.

7. Children

Sogdia is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact
