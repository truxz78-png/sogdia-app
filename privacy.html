Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Sogdia ("the app") is published by DriftforgeDev. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information we collect

Sogdia does not require an account and does not collect, store or sell any personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts or payment details.

2. Affiliate links

Sogdia shows links to third-party partner stores. Some of these are affiliate links: if you make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When you open a partner store, that store and the affiliate network it uses (for example Admitad, Involve Asia or INRDeals) may use cookies or similar technologies to record the visit and the purchase, according to their own privacy policies. Purchases, payments, shipping and returns are handled entirely by each partner store.

3. Automatic translation

To show the app in your language, the text of the app (store names excluded) may be sent to a translation service. No personal information is sent. Translations may be saved on your device to load faster next time.

4. Advertising and tracking

Sogdia does not show third-party ads inside the app and does not use the advertising ID.

5. Children

Sogdia is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
